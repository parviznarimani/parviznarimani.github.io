## Equations as evolving candidates

The lecture represents candidate formulas as expression trees. Selection favors promising candidates; crossover exchanges subtrees, and mutation changes parts of an expression.

## Accuracy is not the only objective

Complexity penalties discourage unnecessarily large formulas. The lecture also describes PySR’s search for a balance between error and expression size.

## What to take away

The search process is iterative rather than magical. Its outcome depends on the allowed operations, the data and the objectives used to compare candidates.
