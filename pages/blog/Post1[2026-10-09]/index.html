Symbolic Regression: Finding Equations in Data

A brief introduction to symbolic regression and why explicit mathematical expressions can help researchers understand data.

From observations to expressions

Symbolic regression searches for a mathematical expression that fits observations. Instead of choosing a fixed equation and tuning its coefficients, it explores possible structures built from permitted operators and variables.

Why the formula matters

The ML4Science lecture emphasizes interpretable, compact models. An explicit expression can expose relationships that are harder to inspect in a complex predictor.

A practical perspective

A useful formula should be treated as a candidate explanation, not automatic proof of a physical law. This distinction matters when translating model output into scientific understanding.
